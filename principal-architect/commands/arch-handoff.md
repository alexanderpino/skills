description: Seed an OpenSpec or spec-kit change from a decided architecture change — derived, never invented
argument-hint: <openspec|speckit> [change-id] — or 'constitution'
---

This command runs the **handoff** workflow of the `principal-architect` skill. Read
`references/workflows/handoff.md` in the skill's directory (next to its SKILL.md) now and
follow it step by step; it is authoritative over anything written here. The seed traces
every statement to the architecture record and marks what the record does not say; the
target tool's own validator remains the gate. `constitution` exports the enterprise
principles (PR.xx) as spec-kit's constitution.

Input:

$ARGUMENTS
