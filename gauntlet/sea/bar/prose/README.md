# The prose bar — a pointer, not a copy

The comparator for the `prose` dimension is **`terrain-architect/references/`**, in this repo.

It is a sibling skill written to the same standard and by the same conventions, and **not by this
run** — which is what makes it a bar rather than a mirror. It satisfies all four intake tests:
external to the loop, openable, unarguable about register and provenance discipline, and
demonstrably reachable, because the `terrain-renderer` chapters are already written against it.

**Nothing is copied here on purpose.** A paraphrased bar drifts every time it is restated, and
drift always makes the bar easier. Open the chapters.

## The checkable properties

A critic given only this path can score a chapter on these. They are properties of the text, so
they are checkable by reading — but each is a **yes/no about a specific sentence**, not a
judgement of quality.

1. **Every claim is derived, cited, or marked open.** There is no fourth category. A number
   standing alone with no derivation beside it and no citation behind it is a defect, however
   plausible it is.
2. **Every figure carries a provenance mark.** The tier is on the claim, not on the chapter. A
   composite tier (`P/D`, `P/?`, `P/synthesis`) means the halves are true of *different parts* of
   the claim, and the entry says which half is which.
3. **`?` is used for a genuinely unresolved quantity, never as a hedge on a guess.** The marker
   means *this run could not close it* — and the corollary the comparator enforces: repeating a
   `?` number without its marker is how an unverified figure becomes a cited one.
4. **Corrections are left visible.** When a chapter overturns something it previously said, the
   old claim, the reason, and the date stay in the text. A silently edited number is
   indistinguishable from one that was always right, and the reader cannot tell which they have.
5. **No claim is stated that no code path demonstrates** — the contract's own rule. Prose that
   describes a mechanism nothing implements is a plan, not a chapter.
6. **A tier is never upgraded to satisfy a question, and a citation is never fabricated.** If a
   claim lands on `?`, the text says so.
7. **A measured figure names what it was measured on**, and says what transfers. In the comparator
   a measured `D` is a property of *that* scene; the mechanism travels, the number does not.

## What this bar cannot cover

**Register, rhythm and whether a chapter is worth reading.** These properties are mechanical by
design, which is their strength as a bar and their limit as a judgement: a chapter can satisfy all
seven and still be unreadable. Prose *craft* is not scorable from this path and must not be
reported as passing because the seven checks passed.

**Correctness of the physics.** That is `gauntlet/sea/bar/physics/answer-key.md`. A chapter can be
impeccably provenance-marked and wrong.

**Whether the figures are the right figures.** Property 2 checks that a mark is present, not that
the mark is accurate. Verifying a tier means going to the source, which is a wave's work, not a
scoring pass.
