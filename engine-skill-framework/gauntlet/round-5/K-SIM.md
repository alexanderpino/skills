### K-SIM-1 · major · missing-contract
- Target: C-PHYS, L36, PHY.ARCH.events, PHY.DYN.parallel, C-DET
- Finding: The C-PHYS summary says contact events are delivered through step-time callbacks "so gameplay can react and modify bodies during the step". Legacy L36 ("Per-body sync callbacks & mid-step world mutation") and PHY.ARCH.events ("queries batched, mutations deferred") say the opposite. An implementer reading the contract can build the pattern the legacy catalogue bans. With parallel islands (PHY.DYN.parallel) such callbacks run on solver threads in nondeterministic order, which breaks any determinism level above none and breaks the restore-and-resimulate conformance.
- Evidence: Jolt's ContactListener may only adjust contact settings (friction, restitution, surface velocity) and reject contacts. It may not add or remove bodies, and its callbacks must be thread-safe. PhysX contact modification works the same way. Box2D v3 returns events after the step.
- Proposed change: Rewrite the C-PHYS clause as: step-time callbacks are contact-modification only (ignore, friction, restitution, one-way and surface velocity), pure functions of contact data. Body mutation goes through the deferred command buffer applied at a named sync point. Reactive events are batched after the step, sorted by canonical body ID whenever the declared level is above none. Update L36's stance_capabilities to cite this split.

### K-SIM-2 · major · missing-contract
- Target: NET.SRV.zoning, NET.SRV.cross-server, C-SHARD, physics-architect, vehicle-physics, character-physics
- Finding: Neither physics-architect nor its experts consume C-SHARD. They are not contributors to NET.SRV.zoning or NET.SRV.cross-server. Nothing owns the physics side of authority transfer. That covers hand-off of a live body's state (velocity, sleep state, joints, attached passengers and cargo) and bodies straddling a server boundary. It also covers ghost or overlap-zone collision against neighbour-owned bodies, and vehicles crossing boundaries at speed. The persistent-world profile is claimed by the AAA server configuration, and the radar cites Star Citizen physics grids as evidence for PHY.ARCH.local-frames.
- Evidence: Star Citizen server meshing and Improbable SpatialOS both hit boundary physics as their hard problem. Sea of Thieves avoids it by sharding whole ships. PHY.ARCH.persistence only covers dehydrate and rehydrate on cell deactivation, not live migration.
- Proposed change: Add capability PHY.ARCH.migration ("live body, joint-graph and controller hand-off across server authority boundaries; boundary ghost-body policy; declare unsupported cases such as cross-boundary constraints"), owned by physics-architect with server-scaleout-persistence and replication. Add C-SHARD? to the consumes of physics-architect, vehicle-physics and character-physics. Add physics-architect as a contributor to NET.SRV.cross-server. Extend C-SHARD with a physics-hand-off record.

### K-SIM-3 · major · missing-contract
- Target: ANM.IK.physical, PHY.TOOL.physics-asset, GAM.MOVE.transitions, C-MOVE, C-CHARCTRL, C-ANIM
- Finding: The capsule to ragdoll to get-up transition has no owner. Three skills touch it: character-physics owns the capsule controller, ik-procedural-animation owns ragdoll and physical animation, and character-movement owns movement modes. GAM.MOVE.transitions covers only mount and vehicle. C-MOVE mentions no ragdoll state. Also unowned are the authority class of a ragdoll (cosmetic client-local versus server-replicated, or restorable under rollback), its LOD and budget, and pose hand-back to the animation graph (get-up blend and ground-orientation choice). NET.REP.physics-bodies covers single bodies, not articulated ragdoll pose.
- Evidence: Hit-reaction and death ragdolls are in every shipped action title, and the switch from a kinematic capsule to simulated bodies is a well-known desync and popping source (UE Chaos ragdoll replication, Overgrowth and Euphoria talks). Whether ragdoll stays cosmetic decides whether it can sit outside C-SNAPSHOT.
- Proposed change: Add GAM.MOVE.ragdoll-transition (owner character-movement, with ik-procedural-animation and character-physics). It declares the state machine, handover of the capsule and body set, and ragdoll authority class (cosmetic by default, using the C-SCALE actuator class rules). Add a ragdoll body budget to PHY.ARCH.budget-degradation. Add a ragdoll-state hand-back clause to C-ANIM.

### K-SIM-4 · major · wrong-owner
- Target: collision-detection, rigid-body-dynamics, physics-2d, C-PHYS (implements)
- Finding: C-PHYS is a needs_implementer contract whose implementers are rigid-body-dynamics and physics-2d. Half of the C-PHYS surface (handle-addressed batched queries, filtering, collider history, shape creation) is owned by collision-detection, which has implements: []. Rigid-body-dynamics implements the contract but owns no query capability. So a check.py "implemented" pass does not show the queries and shapes have an implementer, and the two 3D experts can both claim or both disclaim them.
- Evidence: In Jolt, PhysX and Bullet the query and collision subsystem is a separate module, but the world facade unifies them. The framework's own principle is that the contract owner is not the implementer.
- Proposed change: Set collision-detection implements ["C-PHYS"] and state in the C-PHYS text that rigid-body-dynamics implements bodies, stepping and snapshot while collision-detection implements shapes, queries and collider history. Alternatively, add a C-COLLIDE sub-contract owned by physics-architect.

### K-SIM-5 · minor · overlap
- Target: NET.REP.physics-bodies, NET.PRED.physics, NET.REP.interpolation
- Finding: Three capabilities have two owners for replicated-body presentation. NET.REP.physics-bodies (replication) lists "prop extrapolation" and ownership hand-off. NET.PRED.physics (prediction-rollback) lists "interpolation, extrapolation and smoothing" of replicated dynamic bodies. NET.REP.interpolation is the generic snapshot interpolation. No text says who owns error smoothing and correction policy.
- Evidence: In Rocket League-style networked physics the extrapolation and smoothing policy is one decision, not two. Two owners would each write a client-side body view and their corrections would fight.
- Proposed change: Make NET.PRED.physics own all client-side presentation of replicated bodies (extrapolation, smoothing, snap thresholds). Limit NET.REP.physics-bodies to wire content, priority and ownership hand-off. Say this in the non-responsibilities of both skills.

### K-SIM-6 · minor · maturity-error
- Target: NET.PRED.physics-lockstep, PHY.ARCH.fixed-point-backend, PHY.ARCH.determinism, gap A4
- Finding: NET.PRED.physics-lockstep and PHY.ARCH.fixed-point-backend are labelled E with no dimension split. Gap-analysis A4 says cross-platform float reproducibility has thin production evidence. Established shipped evidence is limited to 2D and fixed-point sims, or same-binary lockstep. General 3D rigid-body lockstep on mixed platforms is not established. NET.PRED.physics-rollback is correctly M.
- Evidence: Photon Quantum ships fixed-point physics. Box2D v3 and Jolt document cross-platform determinism only under fixed compiler and flag constraints. Rapier has an enhanced-determinism mode.
- Proposed change: Keep 2D lockstep and the fixed-point backend at E. Split NET.PRED.physics-lockstep into a 2D or fixed-point E row and a 3D cross-platform-float M row. Add a radar entry with the revisit condition "a shipped title runs 3D rigid-body lockstep across two ISAs".

### K-SIM-7 · minor · missing-contract
- Target: cloth-deformables, PHY.SOFT.ropes, PHY.SOFT.cloth, C-SNAPSHOT, C-STATECLASS
- Finding: cloth-deformables consumes neither C-SNAPSHOT nor C-STATECLASS, and C-DET only optionally. PHY.ARCH.rewind lists only rigid-body-dynamics, prediction-rollback and determinism-replay. Ropes and cables are gameplay-visible and can be rollback-relevant (grapples, bridges, tethers), while character cloth is cosmetic. Nothing declares the class per soft-body kind. Conformance then cannot say whether a rollback configuration must restore a rope.
- Evidence: The framework's own C-SCALE actuator classes, and the requirement that every component has a state class, imply this declaration is mandatory.
- Proposed change: Add C-SNAPSHOT? and C-STATECLASS to cloth-deformables. State in PHY.SOFT.* that cloth and hair are "derived or cosmetic, excluded from hashes", while ropes and softbody are "restorable when gameplay-visible". Add PHY.SOFT.ropes to the NET.PRED.physics-rollback conformance list.

### K-SIM-8 · minor · missing-contract
- Target: C-VEHICLE, C-CROWD, C-MOVE, C-CHARCTRL, C-AI, QA.SIM.deformables
- Finding: The oracle_reference for C-VEHICLE, C-CROWD, C-MOVE, C-CHARCTRL and others is the boilerplate "reference scenes or analytic cases declared in the conformance suite; derived goldens are regression-only". That names no independent oracle, so nothing objective bounds what an oracle author may write. C-PHYS, by contrast, names analytic scenes and Bullet/Jolt differential runs.
- Evidence: Established references exist. For vehicles, use published Pacejka tire curves and the ISO 3888 double lane change and ISO 4138 steady-state circle. For crowds, use the ORCA/RVO antipodal-circle and bottleneck scenarios. For controllers, use analytic slope, step and ledge cases. For deformables, use the cantilever and pendulum-cloth analytic cases.
- Proposed change: Replace the boilerplate strings in those five contracts with named references. Set the C-PHYS differential reference to a backend other than the one shipped.

### K-SIM-9 · minor · overlap
- Target: PHY.FLUID.grid, GAM.SIM.fields, GAM.SIM.cellular, PHY.DYN.buoyancy, C-ENV, water-ocean
- Finding: "Water and fire on a grid" is reachable through four owners. PHY.FLUID.grid owns smoke and fire on a grid. GAM.SIM.fields owns diffusion and cellular spread. GAM.SIM.cellular owns liquid, gas and heat grids. C-ENV water volume comes from water-ocean or voxel providers. The non-responsibilities separate fluid-simulation from systems-simulation only by the words "continuum and particle fluids". Nothing says which representation is gameplay-authoritative, or which one may feed the others.
- Evidence: Noita, Teardown and Oxygen Not Included use cellular or grid authority for gameplay. Niagara Fluids and Flex-class fluids are cosmetic or coupled through one-way forces.
- Proposed change: Add a decision table to the PHY.FLUID.grid text: gameplay-authoritative liquid or fire is cellular (GAM.SIM.cellular or GAM.SIM.fields). PHY.FLUID.* is presentation-grade, with one-way coupling into C-PHYS bodies. C-ENV water volume is authoritative for buoyancy.
