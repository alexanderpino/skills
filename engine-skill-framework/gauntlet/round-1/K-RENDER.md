# K-RENDER · Rendering Critic · Round 1 findings

### K-RENDER-1 · blocker · missing-contract
- Target: direct-lighting-shadows, global-illumination, atmosphere-weather (all provide no contract); consumers translucency-decals (RND.TRANS.lighting), vfx-particles, water-ocean (WLD.ENV.water-render), character-rendering, vegetation-foliage, terrain
- Finding: No contract exposes lighting data or participating-media data. The light lists (clustered or stochastic reservoirs), shadow-map or VSM lookups, probe, radiance-cache or lightmap sampling, sky and environment lighting, and the froxel fog and aerial-perspective volumes all have no contract. `direct-lighting-shadows`, `global-illumination` and `atmosphere-weather` provide nothing (`provides: []`). Yet translucency owns "translucent lighting & volumetric integration", and particles, water, hair, lit fog and cloud shadows must all *sample* that data. `C-RSCENE` only covers proxies, sync, feature registration and views.
- Evidence: In every production forward/deferred hybrid (UE5 `ForwardLightData` + `TranslucencyLightingVolume` + Lumen translucency radiance cache; Frostbite's clustered and froxel volumetrics, Hillaire SIGGRAPH 2015; Decima's volumetrics), the lighting system publishes a sampling interface that 5–10 other features consume. Without an owned contract, parallel agents either reach into GI/shadow internals, which breaks principle §3, or each builds a private light-sampling path, which yields inconsistent lighting between opaque and translucent surfaces. That is a classic visual bug class. Weather wetness and snow (WLD.ENV.weather) also need a material-facing global-parameter channel, and nothing provides one.
- Proposed change: Add `C-LIGHT` (layer 3, owner `direct-lighting-shadows`; requires C-RSCENE) covering "light lists, shadow lookup, light units, many-light sampling API". Add `C-GI` (layer 3, owner `global-illumination`) covering "indirect diffuse/specular sampling for any shading point incl. translucency, probes, sky/environment lighting". Add `C-MEDIA` (layer 3, owner `atmosphere-weather`) covering "aerial perspective, froxel/local media volumes, cloud shadows, weather global material parameters". Make translucency-decals, vfx-particles, water-ocean, character-rendering, vegetation-foliage and terrain consume them. Make atmosphere consume C-LIGHT, and GI consume C-MEDIA? for sky capture.

### K-RENDER-2 · major · missing-contract
- Target: C-TEMPORAL; vfx-particles, translucency-decals, water-ocean, vegetation-foliage, terrain, atmosphere-weather, render-2d-vector, xr-runtime
- Finding: Only direct-lighting-shadows, global-illumination and deformation-skinning consume `C-TEMPORAL`. Several skills that must write motion vectors or reactive/transparency masks into the temporal contract do not consume it: particles, translucency, water, wind-animated vegetation (WPO), displaced terrain, and temporally reprojected clouds. The UI backend must also supply HUD-less or UI-separate inputs for frame generation, and XR spacewarp needs depth and motion-vector submission.
- Evidence: The FSR 2/3 and DLSS integration guides and the XeSS SDK all require per-pixel motion vectors for every moving surface, plus reactive and transparency-composition masks for particles and translucency. Frame generation (DLSS FG, FSR 3 FI) requires HUD-less color or a separate UI surface. Missing vectors on foliage and particles are the most common upscaler ghosting defect in shipped titles. OpenXR `XR_FB_space_warp` requires app motion vectors and depth. Because the data does not declare these dependencies, the closure proof cannot catch them.
- Proposed change: Add `C-TEMPORAL` to the consumes of vfx-particles, translucency-decals, water-ocean, vegetation-foliage, terrain, atmosphere-weather, render-2d-vector (UI compositing for frame generation) and xr-runtime (`C-TEMPORAL?`). Extend the C-TEMPORAL summary with "motion-vector obligations for all moving/animated surfaces; UI/HUD separation for frame generation".

### K-RENDER-3 · major · scale-down
- Target: ray-tracing-infrastructure (profile `aaa`), RND.RT.software, RND.GI.hybrid, RND.LIGHT.rt-shadows, configurations standard-3d / open-world / online-3d / xr-3d
- Finding: HW ray tracing is available only in `aaa-open-world-online`. In `standard-3d` and `open-world`, the established-labelled capability RND.GI.hybrid ("software + hardware RT") and RND.LIGHT.rt-shadows lose their owner of acceleration structures. RND.RT.software, the *software* fallback, also sits inside the aaa-only skill, so the non-RT fallback is absent exactly where it is needed.
- Evidence: In 2025–26, HW RT is present on PS5, PS5 Pro, Xbox Series, Switch 2 and all current desktop GPUs. Titles now *require* it: Indiana Jones and the Great Circle, Doom: The Dark Ages, AC Shadows' RTGI on console. Lumen HW RT is UE5's default on capable hardware. Tying RT to "AAA" is the legacy assumption the brief forbids, and the fallback placement inverts the scale-down logic.
- Proposed change: Retag ray-tracing-infrastructure to `std3d` (still consumed optionally, `C-RT?`, so the mobile and low tiers degrade). Move RND.RT.software and RND.GI.sdf into one owner (see K-RENDER-4) that is present in `mobile3d`/`std3d`. Keep only RND.RT.cluster-blas, RND.RT.ser and RND.RT.omm behind the `aaa` tier as capability flags, not a profile gate on the whole skill.

### K-RENDER-4 · major · overlap
- Target: RND.RT.software (ray-tracing-infrastructure) vs RND.GI.sdf (global-illumination)
- Finding: "Software ray tracing fallback" and "SDF / voxel scene representations for tracing" are the same mechanism, the Lumen-class SW tracing path over mesh or global distance fields and voxels, but they have two owners. Two agents would each build a tracing scene representation. Either the build rules or the update policies would diverge, or GI would reinvent the RT abstraction.
- Evidence: In UE5, "Software Ray Tracing" *is* mesh-SDF plus global-SDF tracing, owned by one system behind the same trace API as HW RT (Wright, "Lumen: Real-time GI in UE5", SIGGRAPH 2022). The same holds for Snowdrop and for AMD's Brixelizer as a drop-in SW tracer.
- Proposed change: Make `C-RT` a "ray query" contract with HW and SW backends. Move RND.GI.sdf to ray-tracing-infrastructure, renamed "SDF / voxel tracing scene (software backend)", merged with RND.RT.software. global-illumination keeps only the GI algorithms and consumes `C-RT?`.

### K-RENDER-5 · major · scale-down
- Target: path-tracing (profile `aaa`), RND.PT.reference, QA.RENDER.reference, RND.MAT.validation, render-validation (profile `client`)
- Finding: The ground-truth oracle that render-validation and the material furnace tests depend on exists only in the aaa configuration. It also requires C-RT (HW RT) outright. So standard-3d, mobile-3d and open-world have no reference renderer, and validation silently degrades to golden images only.
- Evidence: A reference path tracer is a development-time correctness tool, not a shipping feature (Falcor, the UE Path Tracer, and Frostbite's reference mode in Hillaire & de Rousiers "Moving Frostbite to PBR" 2014 are all used to tune every tier). It can run as compute-based traversal without HW RT.
- Proposed change: Split RND.PT.reference into a non-shipping, tool-profile capability present in all 3D configurations (`mobile3d`, `std3d`), consuming `C-RT?` with a compute/SW-trace fallback. RND.PT.realtime stays `aaa`. Either give path-tracing profiles `mobile3d,std3d` with realtime as a tiered capability, or move the reference to render-validation's tool set.

### K-RENDER-6 · major · scale-down
- Target: terrain, water-ocean, vegetation-foliage (profile `openworld`); configurations standard-3d, online-3d, xr-3d, mobile-3d
- Finding: Terrain, water and vegetation exist only when `openworld` is added. A standard-3d racing game, a mid-size action game, an online shooter on outdoor maps, or a mobile 3D game has no owner for heightfield terrain, lakes or rivers, or grass and foliage instancing.
- Evidence: Terrain, water and foliage are baseline for most non-open-world 3D games (e.g. arena shooters, racing, and mobile titles such as Genshin-class games on the mobile tier). "Open world" changes their *scale* (streaming, virtual heightfields, HLOD), not their existence.
- Proposed change: Retag terrain, water-ocean and vegetation-foliage to `mobile3d,std3d`. Mark open-world-only capabilities (virtual heightfield streaming, ocean spectral LOD at world scale, foliage cell streaming) as dependent on `C-WORLD?` (optional), so the small form works without world partition.

### K-RENDER-7 · major · scale-down
- Target: configuration indie-2d / indie-2d-online-moddable (profile `min2d` = "Small 2D / 2.5D"); RND.GEO.fallback, RND.GEO.instancing (gpu-driven-pipeline), RND.TEX.formats, RND.TEX.mip-streaming (texture-streaming-vt), RND.LIGHT.units
- Finding: The min2d profile claims 2.5D, but no skill in it draws a 3D mesh. gpu-driven-pipeline, direct-lighting-shadows and translucency are all mobile3d/std3d. No skill in min2d owns runtime GPU texture formats or texture residency either, because RND.TEX.formats lives in texture-streaming-vt next to VT and sampler feedback. The closure check passes only because these dependencies are implicit rather than declared contracts.
- Evidence: 2.5D games are 3D-rendered with constrained cameras: Hollow Knight: Silksong-class layered scenes, Octopath-style HD-2D, and Ori (the last with unlit/lit meshes). Every 2D game still loads BCn/ASTC textures. Bundling the basic texture path with virtual texturing forces 2D to either carry VT or have no texture owner.
- Proposed change: Split texture-streaming-vt. Either give RND.TEX.formats and RND.TEX.mip-streaming a `client`-profile owner (e.g. gpu-memory-resources, or a new "texture-runtime" capability set), with VT, feedback and NTC as std3d/aaa tiers. Or retag texture-streaming-vt to `client` and declare VT optional. Add a `min2d`-profile "simple forward mesh path" capability (RND.GEO.simple-forward, owner gpu-driven-pipeline, retagged `client` with GPU-driven features as tiers). Alternatively, drop "2.5D" from the min2d description and add a `min25d` profile.

### K-RENDER-8 · major · scale-down
- Target: character-rendering (profile `aaa`), RND.CHAR.skin, RND.CHAR.eyes, RND.CHAR.hair
- Finding: Skin shading, eyes and hair have no owner outside aaa. RND.CHAR.hair covers strand-based hair only (M). The established hair-card technique (alpha-to-coverage or dithered cards with Marschner/Kajiya-Kay shading) and screen-space or pre-integrated SSS have no owner in standard-3d or mobile-3d. Both are required by virtually every character-driven 3D game.
- Evidence: Hair cards are standard in nearly all shipped console/PC titles and are the fallback LOD even in UE5 Groom setups. Separable screen-space SSS (Jimenez et al. 2015) and pre-integrated skin (Penner 2011) are established low-cost paths. Strand hair (Frostbite, UE Groom, Tariq & Bavoil) is the aaa tier.
- Proposed change: Retag character-rendering to `mobile3d,std3d`. Add RND.CHAR.hair-cards (E, owner character-rendering) and rename RND.CHAR.hair to "Strand hair & fur (aaa tier)". Give RND.CHAR.skin an explicit tier ladder (pre-integrated → screen-space → ray-traced/path-traced SSS).

### K-RENDER-9 · major · omission
- Target: RND.RECON (reconstruction-upscaling); xr-3d, mobile-3d, indie-2d configurations
- Finding: The only anti-aliasing capability is TAA/temporal reconstruction. MSAA (including TBDR on-tile resolve), post AA (SMAA/FXAA), alpha-to-coverage, and specular/geometric AA have no owner.
- Evidence: MSAA with on-tile resolve is effectively free on TBDR and is the default on Quest and most mobile forward renderers. Temporal AA is widely avoided in VR because of smearing under reprojection (Meta and Valve rendering guidelines, and the UE mobile forward renderer's default of MSAA). 2D and pixel-art games need non-temporal AA or none. Without an owner, the only path for these configurations is TAA, which is the wrong default for them.
- Proposed change: Add RND.RECON.msaa ("MSAA, on-tile resolve, alpha-to-coverage", E), RND.RECON.post-aa ("SMAA/FXAA-class post AA", E) and RND.RECON.specular-aa (E), all owned by reconstruction-upscaling. Retag reconstruction-upscaling to `client`, so 2D games get pixel-art-safe AA and upscaling.

### K-RENDER-10 · major · scale-down
- Target: RND.RHI.bindless (rhi-core), design principle §6 "Bindless is the default binding model", RND.RHI.backends (WebGPU), PLAT.PAL.web
- Finding: Bindless is mandated as *the* binding model, and the only scale-down path declared is for draw submission (RND.GEO.fallback). WebGPU has no bindless in core (the bindless proposal is still in progress in 2025–26). Many shipping Android Vulkan devices lack adequate descriptor-indexing limits, and WebGL-class limits are worse. No capability owns a slot or descriptor-set binding fallback, or the shader-side abstraction that lets one shader source target both models.
- Evidence: See the WebGPU spec limits (`maxBindGroups` 4, `maxSampledTexturesPerShaderStage` 16) and the Android Vulkan descriptor-indexing coverage in the gpuinfo.org data. Production engines that target both use a dual path: bgfx, Filament, UE's mobile RHI, and Unity's SRP Batcher vs BatchRendererGroup.
- Proposed change: Add RND.RHI.binding-fallback ("Bounded descriptor-set/bind-group binding path for non-bindless tiers", E, owner rhi-core, contributor shader-system). Add RND.SHADER.binding-abstraction (owner shader-system) so binding-model differences are generated, not hand-written. Amend §6 to "bindless default on tiers that support it".

### K-RENDER-11 · major · wrong-boundary
- Target: PLAT.XR.stereo, PLAT.XR.foveation, PLAT.XR.reprojection (xr-runtime) vs RND.ARCH.multiview (render-architect), RND.RECON.vrs (reconstruction-upscaling)
- Finding: xr-runtime owns "stereo/multiview rendering integration" and "foveated rendering" but declares "render pipeline" a non-responsibility, and consumes neither C-RG nor C-TEMPORAL. Foveation is implemented *inside* the render graph: VRS, fragment density maps, multi-resolution/quad-view rendering, or foveated dynamic resolution. Multiview (single-pass stereo via `VK_KHR_multiview` or ViewInstancing) is a render-view property owned by render-architect. Two agents own the same passes.
- Evidence: See the OpenXR `XR_VARJO_quad_views` / `XR_META_foveation_eye_tracked`, `VK_EXT_fragment_density_map`, and D3D12 VRS tier-2 image. In UE and Unity, the renderer implements foveation and instanced stereo, and the XR plugin only supplies the foveation parameters and view poses.
- Proposed change: Keep PLAT.XR.openxr, input, comfort and compositor-side reprojection in xr-runtime. Move stereo/multiview rendering into RND.ARCH.multiview (render-architect). Move foveated-rendering implementation to a new RND.RECON.foveation (M) under reconstruction-upscaling, which already owns VRS. xr-runtime supplies eye-tracking and foveation parameters via a small `C-XRVIEW` contract (view poses, projection, foveation map, spacewarp submission), which render-architect consumes as `C-XRVIEW?`.

### K-RENDER-12 · major · omission
- Target: RND (no owner); global-illumination, direct-lighting-shadows, world-editor-viewport
- Finding: Lighting *authoring and baking production tooling* is missing. Nothing owns lightmap UV generation and atlas packing, lightmap density visualization, the bake orchestration (distributed/incremental GPU baking, bake invalidation), probe-volume and reflection-capture placement tools, light linking/channels, or lighting-scenario (day/night) management. RND.GI.baked covers only the baker algorithm. The brief requires tooling as thorough as runtime.
- Evidence: UE (GPU Lightmass, Lightmass Importance Volumes, lighting scenarios, light channels), Unity (Progressive GPU Lightmapper, Light Probe Groups, Adaptive Probe Volumes with baking sets) and Frostbite (Flux) all ship dedicated lighting-authoring tooling. Lightmap UV/atlas generation (xatlas-class) is distinct from generic mesh optimization.
- Proposed change: Add RND.GI.authoring ("Lighting authoring tools: probe/capture placement, lighting scenarios, density visualization", E, owner global-illumination, contributor world-editor-viewport). Add RND.GI.bake-pipeline ("Incremental/distributed bake orchestration via C-COOK", E, owner global-illumination, contributor content-pipeline-architect). Add CNT.COOK.lightmap-uv ("Lightmap UV generation & atlas packing", E, owner global-illumination per the format-owner rule). Add RND.LIGHT.channels ("Light linking/channels", E, owner direct-lighting-shadows).

### K-RENDER-13 · major · omission
- Target: world-editor-viewport, render-architect (no owner)
- Finding: Editor-only rendering has no owner. This covers GPU picking/ID buffers, selection outlines, wireframe and editor visualization overlays, editor-primitive and gizmo rendering over the scene (depth-aware), asset thumbnail and material-preview rendering, and the editor viewport's multiple simultaneous views. ED.WORLD.viewport is "viewport & camera navigation". RND.ARCH.debug-modes covers buffer visualization only.
- Evidence: Every production editor has a dedicated editor render layer: UE's `EditorPrimitives` pass, `HitProxy` ID buffer, `ThumbnailRenderer` and preview scenes; Unity's SceneView overlays and `AssetPreview`. Without an owner, the editor agent will reach into the renderer or build a second renderer.
- Proposed change: Add RND.ARCH.editor-rendering ("Editor render features: GPU picking, selection outline, editor primitives, preview scenes & thumbnails", E, owner render-architect, contributor world-editor-viewport). world-editor-viewport consumes `C-RSCENE` for it.

### K-RENDER-14 · major · maturity-error
- Target: RND.GRAPH.work-graphs (M), principle §7 listing work graphs as "emerging … may be adopted by ADR"
- Finding: Work graphs are labelled emerging production. As of 2025–26 there is no shipped title using them. They are unavailable on PS5, Switch 2 and Metal. On Vulkan they exist only as the provisional `VK_AMDX_shader_enqueue`. Mesh nodes are still preview-grade. The render-graph owner is being invited to adopt a D3D12-desktop-only execution model into the core scheduler.
- Evidence: D3D12 Work Graphs 1.0 shipped (Agility SDK 1.613, March 2024) only as an API. The public evidence consists of AMD/Microsoft GDC 2024–25 demos and research (e.g. "Real-time procedural generation with work graphs", HPG/GDC 2024), not shipped products.
- Proposed change: Relabel RND.GRAPH.work-graphs to X and remove it from the emerging example list in §7. Require it to be reached through an optional extension point of C-RG, with the indirect/ExecuteIndirect path as the contract baseline.

### K-RENDER-15 · major · omission
- Target: RND (no owner); maturity classes X/S
- Finding: Novel and neural scene representations are absent. There is no capability for 3D Gaussian splatting / radiance-field rendering (rasterized or ray-traced splats, splat import and compression, streaming, compositing with meshes and lighting), and none for neural radiance caching (NRC), which is now a shipped GI/PT accelerator. The mandate names these explicitly. Their absence means the first agent that meets them will adopt them ad hoc, without the maturity gating §7 requires.
- Evidence: Kerbl et al. "3D Gaussian Splatting", SIGGRAPH 2023. 3DGRT (Moenne-Loccoz et al., SIGGRAPH Asia 2024). The Khronos/glTF Gaussian-splat extension work (2025). The UE/Unity splat plugins used in virtual production. NVIDIA RTX Neural Radiance Cache (Müller et al. 2021) shipped in RTX Remix titles (Portal with RTX updates, Half-Life 2 RTX 2025).
- Proposed change: Add RND.GEO.splats ("Gaussian splat / radiance-field rendering & compositing", X, owner gpu-driven-pipeline, contributors ray-tracing-infrastructure, asset-import-interchange). Add CNT.IMP.splats ("Splat/radiance-field capture ingestion", X, owner asset-import-interchange). Add RND.GI.nrc ("Neural radiance caching", M/X, owner global-illumination, contributor ml-inference-runtime), all reached via optional contracts.

### K-RENDER-16 · major · wrong-boundary
- Target: rhi-core (RND.RHI.backends: D3D12, Vulkan, Metal, console APIs, WebGPU in one skill)
- Finding: A single expert owns every graphics backend, plus bindless, PSOs, presentation, crash breadcrumbs and workarounds. The backends differ in literature: Metal argument buffers and residency sets, the NDA console APIs (AGC, D3D12.X, NVN2), and WebGPU's validated safe API. They change at different rates and are validated separately, which meets §1's own split rule. With many autonomous agents, every backend change would serialize through one owner, and console-NDA work cannot share an agent context with public work.
- Evidence: Production engines organize backends as separate modules and teams: UE `D3D12RHI`, `VulkanRHI`, `MetalRHI` and platform RHIs in NDA extensions; the Unity GfxDevice per-API backends; The Forge.
- Proposed change: Keep rhi-core as owner of the abstraction (C-RHI, capability tiers, sync model, validation, workaround registry). Add expert skills `rhi-backend-d3d12`, `rhi-backend-vulkan`, `rhi-backend-metal` and `rhi-backend-web`, plus console backends as NDA extensions under platform-console. Each owns one RND.RHI.backend-<api> capability and provides nothing but an implementation of C-RHI.

### K-RENDER-17 · minor · omission
- Target: fluid-simulation (PHY.FLUID.grid), atmosphere-weather (WLD.ENV.fog), vfx-particles
- Finding: No skill owns rendering of heterogeneous sparse volumes: simulated smoke, fire and explosions, and VDB-authored clouds and effects. fluid-simulation owns the simulation, atmosphere owns global fog and "local media", and VFX owns particles. Who ray-marches and lights a NanoVDB/sparse-volume asset is undefined.
- Evidence: See UE Heterogeneous Volumes (5.3+), NanoVDB (Museth 2021), and EmberGen/VDB workflows in current VFX pipelines.
- Proposed change: Add RND.VFX.volumes ("Sparse/heterogeneous volume rendering & lighting", M, owner vfx-particles, contributors atmosphere-weather, fluid-simulation), consuming C-MEDIA and C-LIGHT from K-RENDER-1.

### K-RENDER-18 · minor · omission
- Target: crowd-simulation (GAM.AI.mass), deformation-skinning, character-rendering
- Finding: Rendering of mass crowds (thousands of animated characters) has no owner. That includes vertex-animation textures, GPU-instanced skinning with animation-texture sampling, and animated impostors. ANM.ARCH.lod covers update rate, not the render representation.
- Evidence: UE MassCrowd uses VAT/Animation-Sharing. Hitman and AC Unity crowd rendering (GDC 2015) are further precedent.
- Proposed change: Add ANM.DEF.crowd ("Instanced crowd deformation: VAT, animation textures, animated impostors", E, owner deformation-skinning, contributors crowd-simulation, virtualized-geometry-lod).

### K-RENDER-19 · minor · maturity-error
- Target: RND.MAT.model (E, "OpenPBR-class, layered"), RND.POST.color (E, "ACES 2.0 class"), RND.SHADER.language (E, "HLSL, Slang"), RND.RHI.backends (E incl. WebGPU), RND.TEX.ntc (M)
- Finding: These labels conflate an established capability with an emerging concrete technique. OpenPBR 1.0 (2024) as a *real-time* layered model is emerging: UE Substrate only reached production status in 5.7. ACES 2.0 output transforms (2024–25) have little game adoption. Slang's production use is emerging relative to HLSL/DXC. WebGPU is labelled emerging elsewhere (PLAT.PAL.web = M) but established here. Neural texture compression has no shipped titles and depends on cooperative-vector/matrix shader features that are still preview, so it is X, not M.
- Evidence: See the OpenPBR 1.0 spec (ASWF, 2024), the ACES 2.0 release (AMPAS 2024), the UE 5.7 release notes (Substrate production-ready), the RTX NTC SDK beta status, and the DirectX Shader Model 6.9/6.10 cooperative-vector/linear-algebra preview history.
- Proposed change: Split the capabilities into established baseline and emerging technique. Examples: RND.MAT.model "layered PBR material model" E plus RND.MAT.openpbr M; RND.POST.color E plus RND.POST.aces2 M; RND.SHADER.slang M; RND.RHI.backend-web M. Relabel RND.TEX.ntc to X.

### K-RENDER-20 · minor · overlap
- Target: RND.RHI.crash (rhi-core, "GPU breadcrumbs & device-lost recovery") vs OBS.CRASH.gpu (crash-diagnostics, "GPU crash dumps")
- Finding: Breadcrumbs and GPU crash dumps come from the same vendor mechanisms (DRED, Aftermath, Radeon GPU Detective, and the console GPU fault APIs), which share one enablement, one marker stream and one symbolication path (shader debug info). Splitting the mechanism across two owners invites two marker systems. GPU-side debugging tools (programmatic PIX/RenderDoc capture, shader-side debug draw for GPU-driven culling) also have no owner beyond GPU printf.
- Evidence: See D3D12 DRED, NVIDIA Nsight Aftermath (markers plus dumps from one integration), and AMD RGD.
- Proposed change: rhi-core owns the whole GPU fault instrumentation (breadcrumbs, markers, dump generation). crash-diagnostics owns only upload, bucketing and triage of the dumps; reword OBS.CRASH.gpu to "GPU crash dump ingestion & triage". Add RND.SHADER.gpu-debug-draw and RND.RHI.capture-integration (E, owners shader-system and rhi-core).
