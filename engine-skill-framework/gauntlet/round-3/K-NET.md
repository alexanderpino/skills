# K-NET — Networking Critic, round 3

Not repeated here because other round-3 critics already report them: PHY.ARCH.stepping vs L30, the C-NETLINK "sessions" naming, and BLD.REL.staged-rollout.

### K-NET-1 · major · wrong-boundary
- **Target:** C-SERVER, dedicated-server, NET.SRV.budgets, NET.SRV.overload, NET.SRV.admin, net-session, replication, coop-3d-listen-client
- **Finding:** C-SERVER mixes two things. One is process and fleet lifecycle (allocate, ready, drain), which is server-only. The other is host authority services: per-connection budgets, overload state and kick/ban. dedicated-server ships only in the `server` target. So coop-3d-listen-client, the P2P host in net-session and the rollback/lockstep hosts get no per-connection quotas, no overload degradation and no kick/ban. replication contributes to NET.SRV.overload and NET.SRV.budgets but does not consume C-SERVER. That means it cannot read the overload state and throttle relevancy, as NET.SRV.overload requires.
- **Evidence:** A listen host is an authoritative server that also renders. In Unreal, the NetDriver connection limits, RPC flood protection and the server-side replication graph run in listen servers as well, and the same holds for Steam P2P hosts. A listen host has less CPU headroom than a dedicated server, so it needs overload degradation (adaptive relevancy, tick dilation) even more. Configuration closure currently hides the gap.
- **Proposed change:** Split C-SERVER. Add C-HOSTAUTH (L3, owner net-session, all targets): per-connection memory/CPU/RPC quotas, overload state and degradation signals, kick/ban and the host-local admin surface. Move NET.SRV.budgets and NET.SRV.overload to net-session, with dedicated-server and replication as contributors. Keep C-SERVER for process and fleet lifecycle only. Add `C-HOSTAUTH?` to the consumes of replication, prediction-rollback and gameplay-architect.

### K-NET-2 · major · missing-contract
- **Target:** C-PREDICT, vfx-particles, audio-content-runtime, animation-runtime, input-devices-haptics, gameplay-camera, ui-architect
- **Finding:** C-PREDICT promises "cosmetic-effect suppression during resim", but only anti-cheat-integrity, gameplay-systems-toolkit, vehicle-physics and character-movement consume it. The presentation owners do not consume C-PREDICT, C-FRAME resim flags or any predicted-event channel: VFX spawns, SFX triggers, animation notifies, haptics, camera shake and UI. No capability owns rollback-aware presentation either: dedup of predicted cosmetic events, cancelling or fading mispredicted cosmetics, and sounds that play from the right offset after a late confirm. In fighting-2d-rollback-client, every 1–8-frame rollback would re-fire hit sparks, sounds and rumble.
- **Evidence:** GGPO-style integrations and fighting-game postmortems (Skullgirls, Mortal Kombat X "8 frames of rollback", GDC 2018) name re-triggered and duplicated audio/VFX as the main presentation bug class. The fix is a separation between simulation and presentation with event keys. Unreal's GAS prediction keys and Overwatch's GDC 2017 netcode talk apply the same idea to predicted cosmetics.
- **Proposed change:** Add NET.PRED.presentation, "Rollback/prediction-aware presentation: predicted cosmetic event keys, dedup on resim, cancel/fade of mispredicted cosmetics, late-confirm offset playback". Owner prediction-rollback; contributors vfx-particles, audio-content-runtime, animation-runtime, input-devices-haptics, gameplay-camera. Add `C-PREDICT?` to the consumes of vfx-particles, audio-content-runtime, animation-runtime, input-devices-haptics and gameplay-camera. Alternatively, expose a resim/confirmed-frame flag on C-FLOW event channels and have those skills consume that.

### K-NET-3 · major · scale-down
- **Target:** server-scaleout-persistence (profiles ["online"]), indie-2d-online-moddable-server, online-3d-server, sandbox-online-server
- **Finding:** server-scaleout-persistence (zoning, meshing, cross-server hand-off, leases, write-behind, idempotent transactions) is in the `online` profile. It therefore ships in every online server, including the 2D indie moddable server and a single-match shooter server. Nothing requires it (dedicated-server consumes C-SHARD only optionally). Its presence invites C-SRVDATA and C-SHARD coupling into small servers, and the scale-down proof cannot show that a single-process match server exists without it.
- **Evidence:** Session-based games (match shooters, co-op, indie survival servers such as Valheim or Terraria) run one process per match and persist through local saves or backend calls. Multi-server authority and transactional persistence are MMO/persistent-world concerns: SpatialOS, Star Citizen, New World. The design principles already separate these by lifecycle, but the profile does not.
- **Proposed change:** Add an addon profile `persistent-world` ("Multi-server or persistent authoritative worlds: zoning, hand-off, server persistence, transactions"). Set server-scaleout-persistence.profiles to ["persistent-world"]. Add it to aaa-open-world-online-* and sandbox-online-server, and remove it from the indie and online-3d servers. Give NET.SRV.transactions a capability-level exception if receipts (PLAT.COMM.receipts) require it on session servers.

### K-NET-4 · major · wrong-owner
- **Target:** NET.DBG.profiler, NET.DBG.visualize, NET.DBG.session-replay, NET.REP.spectator, online-performance (profiles ["online"], consumes C-REP), fighting-2d-rollback-client, rts-2d-massim-client, rts-3d-massim-client
- **Finding:** The network profiler, the prediction-error visualization and session record/replay are all owned by replication, which is `online`-only. Rollback and lockstep configurations therefore have no bandwidth attribution, no prediction/rollback-depth visualization and no network session replay. Spectating in rollback/lockstep games (delayed input-stream spectators) also sits in replication. online-performance is `online`-only and hard-requires C-REP, so no independent performance skill measures input delay, rollback depth or send rate for the families that care most about latency. Yet the M2 gate asserts "8 frames under 150 ms".
- **Evidence:** Rollback titles ship network debug overlays for rollback frames, frame advantage and ping (the GGPO network stats API, the Street Fighter 6 and Guilty Gear Strive netcode displays). RTS engines ship lockstep spectator and observer modes that consume the command stream (StarCraft II, AoE2:DE). These are not state-replication features.
- **Proposed change:** Move NET.DBG.profiler to network-transport ("channel/stream bandwidth attribution; categories registered by replication, prediction-rollback, voice"), with replication and prediction-rollback as contributors. Split NET.DBG.visualize: add NET.DBG.prediction-viz ("rollback depth, frame advantage, misprediction visualization"), owned by prediction-rollback. Add NET.PRED.spectator ("input-stream delayed spectators for lockstep/rollback"), owned by prediction-rollback. Set online-performance.profiles to ["online","online-lockstep","online-rollback"] and change its consumes to C-REP? and C-PREDICT?.

### K-NET-5 · major · scale-down
- **Target:** configurations (no tools configuration for online-rollback, online-lockstep or min2d+online), ED.ARCH.pie-net (contributors dedicated-server, network-transport), milestone M2
- **Finding:** Only online-3d-tools and aaa-open-world-online-tools are tools configurations with networking. Neither indie-2d-tools nor minimal-tools carries a netcode profile. So multiplayer PIE, network emulation and the netcode debug tools are never proven for rollback, lockstep or 2D online. M2 claims fighting-2d-rollback-client without its tools, and rts-2d-massim-client arrives in M4 with no tools configuration at all. ED.ARCH.pie-net names dedicated-server (server-target only) and network-transport as contributors, but not net-session (hosting modes, in-process vs multi-process instances) or prediction-rollback (deterministic multi-instance, sync-test mode).
- **Evidence:** Rollback and lockstep iteration depends on in-editor two-instance sessions and "sync test" (GGPO SyncTestSession: resimulate every frame and compare checksums) far more than server-authoritative games do. Unity Multiplayer Play Mode and Unreal PIE with multiple clients are standard editor features. The framework's own rule that "tooling is as thorough as runtime" is not proven for these families.
- **Proposed change:** Add configurations `fighting-2d-rollback-tools` (min2d+online-rollback, tools, target_platforms pc/console) and `rts-massim-lockstep-tools` (min2d+massim+online-lockstep, tools). Claim them in M2 and M4. Change the ED.ARCH.pie-net contributors to net-session, prediction-rollback, network-transport and dedicated-server. Add NET.PRED.sync-test ("per-frame resimulate-and-compare sync-test mode"), owned by prediction-rollback with determinism-replay as contributor.

### K-NET-6 · major · omission
- **Target:** NET.SESS.handshake, modding-ugc, XC.EXT.mod-loading, untrusted input `mods` (trust hostile-local), indie-2d-online-moddable-client/-server, BLD.REL.dlc
- **Finding:** Nothing owns session content-set agreement. That covers the server's mod list, DLC and entitlement content, and cooked content versions checked against each client at join: match, download on demand, refuse, or run with a subset. Server-pushed mod download on join is also unowned. The `mods` input is registered only as hostile-local, but content pushed by a remote server is hostile-remote. NET.SESS.handshake mentions "capability negotiation" only generically, and XC.EXT.mod-compat is about patches, not sessions.
- **Evidence:** Moddable online games all implement this as a distinct subsystem: Minecraft server resource packs, Garry's Mod/Source FastDL and Workshop auto-download, Arma mod-key signature checks, Unreal pak/mount checks with CheckDependenciesOnly. Console certification restricts downloading user content from peers. Mixed-DLC sessions (map-pack ownership) are a common live-ops requirement.
- **Proposed change:** Add NET.SESS.content-set ("Session content-set negotiation: mod/DLC/content-version manifest match at join, server-directed download, subset play, refusal reasons"). Owner net-session; contributors modding-ugc, packaging-release-patching, platform-services. Add a C-NETSESSION clause for content-set results, and add C-NETSESSION? to modding-ugc.consumes. Register the untrusted input `server-pushed-content` (validating owner modding-ugc, parser_owners package-formats-vfs, trust hostile-remote, limits size/signature/permissions).

### K-NET-7 · major · missing-contract
- **Target:** C-REP, replication, NET.ARCH.versioning, QA.FUNC.version-skew, BLD.REL.compat
- **Finding:** NET.ARCH.versioning sets policy ("crossplay patch skew, rolling server deploys"), BLD.REL.compat emits manifests and QA tests the skew matrix. Nothing implements wire-level compatibility of the replicated schema: layout hashes per replicated type, negotiated property/RPC layouts across versions, and tolerant decode of unknown fields. C-REP's summary has no versioning clause, and replication owns no compatibility capability. CORE.SER.evolution covers stored data, not the per-connection negotiated bitstreams that replication hand-packs.
- **Evidence:** Unreal's NetFieldExport groups and replay backward compatibility, plus the network checksum "version mismatch" handshake. Protobuf/FlatBuffers-style field tolerance in live-service protocols. Crossplay titles run console certification patch lag (days) against PC, which requires N−1 protocol support. Without an implementation owner, "exact-build version lock" (L45) comes back by default.
- **Proposed change:** Add NET.REP.compat ("Replicated schema layout hashing, per-connection layout negotiation and tolerant decode across the NET.ARCH.versioning window, incl. replays"). Owner replication; contributors serialization-schema, network-architect. Append "schema layout negotiation within the compatibility window" to the C-REP summary. Add NET.REP.compat to L45 stance_capabilities.

### K-NET-8 · major · omission
- **Target:** online-async profile, NET.ARCH.async, mobile-async-client (only configuration), determinism-replay
- **Finding:** online-async has a client configuration only. The strongest production pattern for competitive async play is validating a client-submitted result by deterministic re-simulation in a backend worker: attack replays, ghost races, puzzle solutions, leaderboard runs. That pattern needs the engine simulation running headless on server-host. There is no capability for it and no headless-client/server configuration for async, and XC.SEC.score-integrity names no re-simulation mechanism.
- **Evidence:** Supercell titles (Clash of Clans, Clash Royale) validate battles by server-side deterministic simulation. Speedrun and leaderboard verification in Trackmania replays ghost inputs server-side. This is also the only robust anti-cheat for async PvP, where the client holds full state.
- **Proposed change:** Add NET.ARCH.async-validation ("Server-side deterministic re-simulation of submitted async results/inputs in a headless worker"). Owner determinism-replay; contributors online-services-liveops, anti-cheat-integrity. Add configuration `mobile-async-validator` (profiles minimal+online-async, target headless-client, platforms server-host), claimed in M2. Add NET.ARCH.async-validation as a contributor route for XC.SEC.score-integrity.

### K-NET-9 · major · omission
- **Target:** dedicated-server.consumes (C-LIVE required), NET.SESS.host-mode, PLAT.SVC.matchmaking, BLD.REL.end-of-service
- **Finding:** dedicated-server hard-requires C-LIVE, so a server process cannot be built without the live-ops backend integration. Player-hosted, community and LAN dedicated servers are not a named hosting mode, and nothing owns server-list/browser registration and query. BLD.REL.end-of-service promises "community server release", but no capability makes a backend-free server possible.
- **Evidence:** Self-hosted dedicated servers are a primary distribution model for survival, sandbox and indie online games: Minecraft, Valheim, Rust, ARK, Terraria, Factorio. Examples include the Steam master-server query protocol (A2S), server browsers, and the backend-free fallbacks after shutdowns (e.g. GameSpy's shutdown in 2014).
- **Proposed change:** Change `C-LIVE` to `C-LIVE?` in dedicated-server.consumes. Extend NET.SESS.host-mode to "(dedicated service-managed, dedicated self-hosted/community, listen, P2P host, relay-hosted)". Add NET.SESS.server-browser ("Server registration, listing & query (backend or master-server/LAN), self-hosted server auth without first-party backend"). Owner net-session; contributors online-services-liveops, network-transport.

### K-NET-10 · minor · missing-contract
- **Target:** untrusted-inputs.json, replication.consumes, C-INTEGRITY, NET.REP.rpc
- **Finding:** The untrusted-input registry has `replicated-state` and `input-commands` but no entry for client→server RPCs and reliable events. RPC arguments are the largest server attack surface, and they are neither input frames nor replicated state. replication, which owns RPCs, does not consume C-INTEGRITY, so no RPC validator registration path exists outside movement and abilities.
- **Evidence:** Unreal's `WithValidation` server RPCs and the long history of exploits through unvalidated server RPCs (item duplication, teleport, admin-function reachability) in live titles.
- **Proposed change:** Register `client-rpcs` (validating_owner replication, parser_owners [], trust hostile-remote, limits "per-RPC rate/arg bounds/authority"). Add `C-INTEGRITY?` to replication.consumes. Append "per-RPC validator & authority declaration" to the C-REP summary.

### K-NET-11 · minor · obsolete-assumption
- **Target:** NET.TRANS.web, NET.TRANS.quic, L42
- **Finding:** NET.TRANS.web lists WebSocket (TCP) as an equal, established browser transport for real-time play in indie-2d-online-web-client. That is L42 (TCP/all-reliable for real-time state), and NET.TRANS.web is not among L42's stance_capabilities, so nothing forces an ADR. NET.TRANS.quic is still "evaluation" (M), although WebTransport datagrams and WebRTC data channels are the production answer for unreliable browser traffic.
- **Evidence:** TCP head-of-line blocking under loss makes WebSocket state sync stutter. WebTransport has shipped in Chromium since 2022 and in Firefox since 2023, and WebKit has an implementation in progress (an Interop focus area). Browser action games (e.g. the .io genre moving to WebRTC/WebTransport) use unreliable channels.
- **Proposed change:** Rename NET.TRANS.web to "Browser transports: WebTransport datagrams and WebRTC unreliable data channels preferred; WebSocket fallback for async/turn-based or by ADR". Add NET.TRANS.web to L42 stance_capabilities. Rename NET.TRANS.quic to "QUIC/WebTransport as a native transport backend".

### K-NET-12 · minor · omission
- **Target:** PLAT.SVC.matchmaking, network-transport
- **Finding:** Nothing owns client-side latency/QoS measurement to candidate regions or data centres (ping beacons, UDP QoS probes, relay-site latency). Matchmaking and region selection need it as input.
- **Evidence:** AWS GameLift/Amazon GameLift ping beacons, PlayFab QoS servers, Steam Datagram Relay ping-location estimates. Matchmakers require client-measured latency per region.
- **Proposed change:** Add NET.TRANS.qos-probe ("Region/data-centre/relay latency & loss probing for matchmaking input"). Owner network-transport; contributor online-services-liveops.

### K-NET-13 · minor · dependency-error
- **Target:** prediction-rollback.consumes, NET.SESS.baseline, NET.SESS.reconnect
- **Finding:** prediction-rollback owns lockstep and rollback but does not consume C-NETSESSION. Session start-frame/seed agreement, peer join and leave, and reconnect in input-exchange families therefore have no contract path. NET.SESS.baseline lists only replication as contributor, which is absent in lockstep/rollback configurations. Late join and reconnect for lockstep need a C-SNAPSHOT-based baseline plus a command-stream catch-up.
- **Evidence:** Lockstep reconnect in RTS games (AoE2:DE, StarCraft II rejoin) is implemented as snapshot or replay fast-forward followed by live command catch-up. Rollback sessions agree on a start frame and on the input delay at handshake.
- **Proposed change:** Add `C-NETSESSION` to prediction-rollback.consumes. Add prediction-rollback and determinism-replay as contributors to NET.SESS.baseline and NET.SESS.reconnect, and rename NET.SESS.baseline to "Join-in-progress baseline (replicated snapshot or C-SNAPSHOT + command catch-up)".

### K-NET-14 · minor · omission
- **Target:** net-session, GAM.FW.local-players, NET.SESS.join
- **Finding:** Online sessions with several local players on one connection (split-screen online, guest sign-ins) are unowned. That includes per-local-player identity and auth, sub-connection IDs, and per-player input-command streams.
- **Evidence:** Console certification and the Halo/Call of Duty/Rocket League split-screen online modes. Unreal child connections for split-screen players.
- **Proposed change:** Add NET.SESS.local-players ("Multiple local players per connection: per-player identity/auth, sub-connection IDs, per-player command streams"). Owner net-session; contributors gameplay-architect, platform-services, input-system.

### K-NET-15 · minor · other
- **Target:** critics.json K-NET.scope
- **Finding:** The K-NET scope leaves out skills that own netcode-critical stances: physics-architect and rigid-body-dynamics (PHY.ARCH.rewind, NET.PRED.physics), physics-2d (PHY.2D.determinism for rollback), frame-orchestration (NET.ARCH.tick, CORE.FRAME.headless), crowd-simulation (lockstep mass sim), modding-ugc (server mods), platform-web (browser transports), audio-dsp-mixing (VOIP path), platform-server-host and online-performance.
- **Evidence:** Of the round-3 defects, PHY.ARCH.stepping (variable timestep, which breaks rollback) sits in physics-architect, outside the K-NET scope. The netcode stages S1–S4 would not review it.
- **Proposed change:** Add physics-architect, rigid-body-dynamics, physics-2d, frame-orchestration, crowd-simulation, modding-ugc, platform-web, audio-dsp-mixing, platform-server-host and online-performance to K-NET.scope.
