# K-SIM — Physics & Simulation Critic, round 3

### K-SIM-1 · major · obsolete-assumption
- **Target:** PHY.ARCH.stepping, physics-architect, L30, CORE.FRAME.fixed-step, K-SIM critic checks
- **Finding:** The physics lead's own stepping capability reads "Variable-timestep stepping on the render frame delta & frame sync" (maturity E). That is legacy pattern L30 ("Frame-coupled variable-timestep simulation", detection "simulation stepped by render delta") stated as an established default for the physics lead. The skill's SKILL.md would inherit the anti-pattern that `legacy-patterns.json` exists to remove. Nothing owns fixed-step sub-stepping and per-subsystem physics rates either, such as tire/drivetrain integration at several hundred Hz or solver sub-steps per fixed tick.
- **Evidence:** Fiedler, "Fix Your Timestep!" (2004). Box2D v3 soft-step and XPBD "small steps" (Macklin et al., 2019) both assume a fixed dt with sub-steps. Jolt and Chaos async physics step at a fixed rate with interpolation. Racing sims run tire models at 300–1000 Hz inside a fixed game tick. With variable dt, snapshot/resim (C-PHYS conformance), lockstep and C-DET cannot work.
- **Proposed change:** Rename PHY.ARCH.stepping to "Fixed-step physics stepping within the C-FRAME fixed-step domain: solver sub-steps, per-subsystem sub-rates (vehicles, cloth), catch-up/spiral-of-death clamps, render interpolation hand-off" and keep frame-orchestration as contributor. Add PHY.ARCH.stepping to L30.stance_capabilities. Add the K-SIM check "no simulation stepped by render delta; sub-rates declared".

### K-SIM-2 · major · dependency-error
- **Target:** milestones.json M2 contracts_frozen (C-PHYS, C-MOVE, C-PREDICT, C-ENV), M3 skills (collision-detection, rigid-body-dynamics, character-physics, vehicle-physics, water-ocean, terrain), M4 (dedicated-server)
- **Finding:** C-PHYS, C-MOVE and C-PREDICT freeze at M2, when physics-2d is the only physics implementer. All 3D physics experts arrive at M3 and dedicated-server at M4. C-PHYS's frozen surface already names 3D-only features that nobody will have implemented or consumed at freeze: collider history for rewind, local simulation spaces, 3D character controllers, world instances for server density. C-PREDICT freezes before any 3D networked physics or server lag compensation exists. C-ENV (water height/velocity for buoyancy, ground surface) freezes before its providers terrain, water-ocean and atmosphere-weather (M3). check.py only proves that an owner and some consumer exist, so a 2D-only proof satisfies it.
- **Evidence:** A contract frozen from one dimensionality needs breaking revisions later. 3D contact manifolds, CCD sweeps with rotation, and capsule-controller sensing on steps, slopes and ledges have no 2D analogue that could validate the API.
- **Proposed change:** Move C-PHYS, C-MOVE, C-PREDICT and C-ENV from M2 contracts_frozen to M3 (C-PREDICT to M4, after dedicated-server and the lag-compensation shooter exit). Alternatively, split out 3D extension contracts that freeze at M3/M4. Add a milestone rule that a contract freezes only after every implementer class it names (2D and 3D physics backend, each C-ENV provider kind) has arrived.

### K-SIM-3 · major · overlap
- **Target:** C-PHYS summary, C-MOVE, character-physics, character-movement, GAM.MOVE.modes, GAM.MOVE.root-motion
- **Finding:** C-PHYS (owner physics-architect) includes "character controllers (movement modes, move requests, root-motion hand-off)". C-MOVE (owner character-movement) is "Movement modes, move requests, resimulatable move API, root-motion hand-off". Two contracts owned in two workstreams (simulation, gameplay), with two conformance suites and two oracle authors, define the same API. character-physics routes "Movement modes" to character-movement, and GAM.MOVE.root-motion gives root-motion authority to character-movement. The C-PHYS text contradicts both.
- **Evidence:** Production splits a physics collide-and-slide primitive (Jolt CharacterVirtual, PhysX CCT) from gameplay movement modes (UE CharacterMovementComponent / Mover). Merging the two is the known source of "who owns step-up/root motion" rework.
- **Proposed change:** Change the C-PHYS text to "character controller primitive (shape sweep collide-and-slide, ground/step/slope sensing, platform riding, depenetration)". Remove movement modes, move requests and root-motion hand-off from C-PHYS. They stay only in C-MOVE.

### K-SIM-4 · major · missing-contract
- **Target:** C-PHYS, physics-2d, rigid-body-dynamics, collision-detection, PHY.ARCH.build-integrate, PHY.ARCH.middleware-layer, gap B6/B23
- **Finding:** C-PHYS is one contract with no implementer structure. In practice it has alternative backends: an in-house 2D solver (physics-2d), in-house 3D (collision-detection + rigid-body-dynamics), or middleware (Jolt, PhysX, Havok, Box2D per B6/B23). Unlike C-RHI and C-PAL, it is not `needs_implementer`, so no configuration proves it has a physics backend. It is also not `boundary`, so it has no verified test double. Gameplay, movement, AI and netcode tests therefore depend on a real solver, and they cannot be run against a fake while physics is swapped between middleware versions. The contract also does not say whether it is dimension-generic: 2D APIs (Vec2, angle, 2D shapes) and 3D APIs differ. physics-2d is tagged only `min2d`, so a lite3d/std3d 2.5D or 3D-rendered 2D-physics game cannot select it. The 3D workaround, PHY.DYN.dof-lock, is a different solver.
- **Evidence:** Unity and Godot expose separate Physics2D/Physics3D APIs. The UE Chaos/PhysX era showed that a physics interface layer with multiple backends is what makes a middleware swap feasible.
- **Proposed change:** Set C-PHYS `needs_implementer: true`, with `implements: ["C-PHYS"]` on physics-2d and on rigid-body-dynamics (or on an explicit middleware-adapter module under physics-architect). Add `boundary: true, test_double: {kind: "scripted kinematic world with deterministic query/contact injection", owner: "physics-architect"}`. Either split C-PHYS into dimension-neutral handles/events/stepping plus C-PHYS2D/C-PHYS3D shape and query extensions, or declare the dimension as a world-creation parameter. Add an opt-in add-on (e.g. `physics2d`) so physics-2d can join lite3d/std3d configurations.

### K-SIM-5 · major · omission
- **Target:** PHY.TOOL.visual-debugger, physics-tools (kind tool, targets [tools]), C-REPLAY stream list, visual-debugging-tools
- **Finding:** "Physics visual debugger & recorder" is owned by a tools-only skill. Nothing captures physics frames on the running client, headless or server build: bodies, contacts, queries, solver islands and sleep state. C-REPLAY registers input, replication, visual-log, animation-debug and external streams, but no physics stream. Server-side physics divergence and rollback mispredictions cannot be recorded where they happen.
- **Evidence:** The PhysX Visual Debugger transport and Chaos Visual Debugger recording live in the runtime SDK, can run on dedicated servers, and are viewed in a separate tool. Jolt ships a runtime DebugRendererRecorder. The same split already exists here for NET.DBG.session-replay (runtime) versus tools.
- **Proposed change:** Add PHY.ARCH.debug-capture "Runtime physics debug capture stream (bodies, contacts, queries, islands, per-step timings) on all targets, zero-cost when compiled out", owned by physics-architect with contributors determinism-replay and visual-debugging-tools. Add "physics debug" to the C-REPLAY stream list. Rename PHY.TOOL.visual-debugger to the viewer/inspector only, and have physics-tools consume C-REPLAY non-optionally.

### K-SIM-6 · major · omission
- **Target:** PHY.ARCH.streaming, destruction-fracture (consumes), GAM.SAVE.world-state, WLD.PART.activation, physics-architect (consumes)
- **Finding:** No capability owns physics state across cell deactivation and saves. When a cell streams out, the physics world must dehydrate moved dynamic props, sleeping state, broken constraints and destruction debris, and restore them on reactivation or load. PHY.ARCH.streaming covers only activation and insertion. physics-architect does not consume C-SAVE and consumes C-WORLD only optionally. destruction-fracture consumes none of C-WORLD, C-SAVE, C-SNAPSHOT or C-SIGNIF. Destroyed buildings therefore reappear intact, debris is invisible to rollback/resim (the body count changes mid-resim), and debris has no significance-driven LOD.
- **Evidence:** In Skyrim and Zelda: BotW/TotK, dynamic prop positions persist through cell unload and save. Battlefield and Teardown persist destruction state. Rollback worlds must snapshot bodies spawned during the resim window.
- **Proposed change:** Add PHY.ARCH.persistence "Physics state dehydrate/rehydrate on cell deactivation and save (moved dynamics, sleep, broken joints, fracture state) as C-SAVE world-state deltas", owned by physics-architect with contributors persistence-save, world-architect and destruction-fracture. Add C-SAVE? and C-WORLD? to physics-architect. Add C-WORLD?, C-SAVE?, C-SNAPSHOT? and C-SIGNIF? to destruction-fracture.

### K-SIM-7 · major · omission
- **Target:** CORE.MATH (math-simd-numerics), CORE.MATH.deterministic (E), PHY.ARCH.determinism, PHY.2D.determinism, gap A4, fighting-2d-rollback-client, lite-3d-mobile-online-client, rts configurations
- **Finding:** Gap A4 tells skills to "decide per subsystem between fixed-point, restricted float and same-binary", but no capability anywhere owns fixed-point numerics or fixed-point (or soft-float) physics. At the same time, CORE.MATH.deterministic, "Cross-platform reproducible math library", is labelled E, which contradicts A4's own evidence that cross-platform float determinism is weakly demonstrated in production. The configurations include crossplay pc+console rollback and mobile online, where cross-platform lockstep or rollback determinism is required.
- **Evidence:** Photon Quantum ships a fixed-point deterministic physics engine used in commercial crossplay titles. Mobile lockstep crossplay games commonly use fixed-point. Jolt's cross-platform determinism mode (2023) is recent and restricts compiler flags and instruction sets.
- **Proposed change:** Add CORE.MATH.fixed-point "Fixed-point / soft-float numeric types and deterministic transcendental functions" (E, math-simd-numerics, contributor determinism-replay). Add PHY.ARCH.fixed-point-backend "Fixed-point physics backend option for cross-platform lockstep/rollback" (E, physics-architect, contributors physics-2d and rigid-body-dynamics). Relabel CORE.MATH.deterministic to M, add a radar entry with fallback "same-binary determinism + fixed-point", and name both capabilities in L47 or a new legacy entry.

### K-SIM-8 · major · dependency-error
- **Target:** fluid-simulation (consumes, purpose), PHY.FLUID.cellular, GAM.SIM.fields, systems-simulation, WLD.ENV.water-interaction, rts-2d-massim-client, rts-3d-massim-client
- **Finding:** The `massim` profile places fluid-simulation in the lockstep configurations rts-2d-massim-client and rts-3d-massim-client. It owns PHY.FLUID.cellular, labelled "deterministic", yet consumes neither C-DET, C-TASK nor C-SNAPSHOT (it consumes only C-PHYS, C-RG?, C-RSCENE? and C-ENV?). The boundary is also doubled twice. PHY.FLUID.cellular ("liquid/gas/heat grids") and GAM.SIM.fields ("diffusion, influence, cellular spread, SIMD/GPU-capable, deterministic") each build a parallel deterministic grid substrate. fluid-simulation's purpose claims "shallow-water simulation", but WLD.ENV.water-interaction is owned by water-ocean.
- **Evidence:** In Oxygen Not Included, gas, liquid and heat run on one grid substrate. A lockstep desync from a fluid grid that never followed C-DET's ordered-reduction rules is exactly what L47 targets.
- **Proposed change:** Add C-DET, C-TASK and C-SNAPSHOT? to fluid-simulation.consumes. Make GAM.SIM.fields the single grid substrate: fluid-simulation implements the material/fluid rules on it and systems-simulation owns storage, stepping and tiling. Add a contributor link, or move PHY.FLUID.cellular's heat/gas diffusion to GAM.SIM.fields. Remove "shallow-water simulation" from the fluid-simulation purpose, or route it to water-ocean in non_responsibilities.

### K-SIM-10 · minor · omission
- **Target:** QA.SIM (simulation-validation), PHY.DEST.*, PHY.FLUID.*, GAM.SIM.*, PHY.COL.ccd, PHY.COL.queries
- **Finding:** QA.SIM has validation runs for rigid stability, vehicles, deformables, mass agents and controllers. It has none for destruction (fracture determinism, debris budgets, replicated consistency), fluids and systems simulation (mass/volume conservation, stability at max dt, desync lanes), or collision robustness (tunneling at speed thresholds for CCD, GJK/EPA degenerate-input metamorphic tests, query correctness against brute force).
- **Evidence:** Tunneling and degenerate-contact bugs are the most common physics regressions and can be expressed as differential or metamorphic oracles, the taxonomy in QA.STRAT.oracles.
- **Proposed change:** Add QA.SIM.collision "CCD tunneling & query-correctness differential/metamorphic runs" (contributor collision-detection, physics-2d), QA.SIM.destruction (contributor destruction-fracture) and QA.SIM.fluids-fields "conservation, stability and desync lanes for fluid and field simulation" (contributors fluid-simulation, systems-simulation).

### K-SIM-11 · minor · omission
- **Target:** untrusted-inputs.json, PHY.COL.runtime-build, WLD.VOX.edits, sandbox-online-server
- **Finding:** On servers, runtime collision generation is driven by remote players' voxel or construction edits and by UGC meshes. The registry has no entry for this input. Pathological geometry (degenerate triangles, huge extents, triangle counts that make the broadphase or convex decomposition explode) is a server CPU denial-of-service with no validating owner or limits.
- **Evidence:** Sandbox servers such as Minecraft and Space Engineers rate-limit and bound per-player edit and collision-rebuild work. NET.SRV.budgets covers generic client-triggered work, not geometry validity.
- **Proposed change:** Add input "runtime-collision-geometry" with validating_owner collision-detection, parser_owners [voxel-worlds, modding-ugc], trust hostile-remote, mode harness, and limits "vertex/triangle count, extents, degeneracy, rebuild time per tick".

### K-SIM-12 · minor · maturity-error
- **Target:** PHY.DEST.runtime
- **Finding:** "Runtime fracture & debris management (large-scale runtime fracture per ADR)" is labelled E. Pre-fractured geometry collections with debris management are established. Procedural fracture at impact time and large-scale runtime fracture are not established, and the ADR hedge in the name hides that.
- **Evidence:** Chaos Destruction and Frostbite destruction use pre-fractured assets. Teardown's runtime destruction is voxel-based. There is little shipped evidence for real-time Voronoi fracture at scale.
- **Proposed change:** Split into PHY.DEST.runtime "Pre-fractured destruction & debris management" (E) and PHY.DEST.procedural "Runtime procedural fracture at impact" (M), and add a radar entry with fallback "pre-fractured assets".

### K-SIM-13 · minor · omission
- **Target:** ANM.IK.physical, ANM.SYN.learned, radar.json
- **Finding:** Physically simulated characters driven by learned policies (RL controllers that output joint torques) have no capability or radar entry. ANM.SYN.learned covers kinematic neural controllers only, and ANM.IK.physical covers classic active ragdoll. Physics–animation coupling therefore has no experimental lane or fallback.
- **Evidence:** DeepMimic (Peng et al., SIGGRAPH 2018); Ubisoft La Forge SuperTrack (SIGGRAPH Asia 2021); vendor physics-based character SDK demos.
- **Proposed change:** Add ANM.IK.learned-physics "Learned physics-based character controllers (policy → joint targets)" (X, ik-procedural-animation, contributors rigid-body-dynamics and ml-inference-runtime, profile experimental) and a radar entry with fallback "active ragdoll with PD motors (ANM.IK.physical)".

### K-SIM-14 · minor · other
- **Target:** critics.json K-SIM (scope, checks)
- **Finding:** K-SIM's scope omits the owners of projectiles, hit detection and ballistics (gameplay-systems-toolkit) and of local avoidance (navigation-pathfinding). Both are simulation-schedule and determinism participants. Its checks do not cover fixed-step stepping, snapshot/resim participation or tier-transition handlers, which is how K-SIM-1 went unnoticed.
- **Evidence:** GAM.SYS.projectiles performs batched sweeps with server rewind. GAM.AI.local-avoidance moves agents every tick under C-DET in lockstep configurations.
- **Proposed change:** Add gameplay-systems-toolkit and navigation-pathfinding to K-SIM.scope. Add the checks "fixed-step only, sub-rates declared", "snapshot/resim participation and cost declared" and "C-SIGNIF tier-transition handlers declared".
