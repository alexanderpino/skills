# ADR template clarity rubric — frozen at intake

Judges the Context / Decision / Consequences sections of an ADR *template*:
whether the template's own instructional text tells a filler exactly what to
write, with no guessing.

1. **Context does not leak the solution.** Describes forces, constraints and
   tensions only.
2. **Decision demands one committed choice, not a menu.** Active voice,
   singular commitment, and alternatives named with a stated reason each lost.
3. **Consequences requires both directions, unprompted.** Negative/costs/risks
   is its own required subsection, not an afterthought.
4. **No ambiguous placeholder.** Every blank states what kind of content goes
   there and roughly how much.
5. **Self-contained.** A filler should not need a second document to know what
   a section wants.

Severity: `major` — a filler would likely produce a wrong or incomplete
section from this instruction alone. `minor` — usable, but a filler would
plausibly ask a clarifying question. `none` — nothing left to tighten.
